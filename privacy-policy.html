Privacy Policy — Advance Drawing Studio

Package ID: com.advancedrawing.studio • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Overview & No Account Required

Advance Drawing Studio ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. You can use the App without creating an account, and we do not operate developer servers that collect or store your name, phone number, address, or drawings.

2. On-Device Image Processing & Local Storage

Photos you capture, select, crop, edit, or trace are processed directly on your device using OpenCV and Google ML Kit. Your saved artworks in My Gallery, along with your coin balance, language preference, and theme settings, are stored locally in your device's private storage and are never uploaded to our servers.

3. App Permissions We Use

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you grant permission to display live camera AR tracing over paper or take a reference photo. Camera frames are analyzed in real time in memory for surface tracking and are never recorded or uploaded.
  • Photo & File Selection: Uses Android's system picker only when you explicitly import an image or ZIP file.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to load Google AdMob ads, perform optional web image searches you request, and verify Google Play in-app purchases.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App uses trusted third-party services that may collect standard technical or advertising information as governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Displays App Open, Banner, and Rewarded Interstitial ads (unless disabled with Coin Ad-Free passes or Lifetime VIP). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes in-app purchases (Coin packs and Permanent Ad-Free) securely via Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Microsoft Bing Images (In-App Search): If you choose to browse online sketches inside the App, search terms are sent directly to Bing Images under Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Data Sharing, Retention & Deletion

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Local drawings remain on your device until you delete them in My Gallery or uninstall the App. Drawings are shared only when you explicitly tap Share or Export ZIP.

6. Children's Privacy

Advance Drawing Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can assist.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as the App evolves. Any updates will appear on this page and inside the App with an updated Effective Date.
